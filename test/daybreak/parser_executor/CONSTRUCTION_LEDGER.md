| P6 | `KNOWN_OR_ACCEPTED` | Existing `CalldataEncodingPoC::test_limitation_nestedMultiSendPassesGuardButCannotExecute` and pre-tag disposition B5 exactly duplicate it: the guard accepts, pinned CallOnly reverts atomically on operation 1, and the target is never called. |

P1's executable refinement stores the trigger authorization in the relay after the owner has signed, instead of embedding it in the signed batch. The entry point and state sequence remain the frozen construction, while this chronology demonstrates that the attacker needs the panic identity plus an already-signed transaction, not post-signature Safe quorum.
